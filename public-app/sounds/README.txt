Reveal sound
------------
Drop the sound designer's file here as  reveal.mp3  (short, about 1.5-2.5 s, normalised to about -16 LUFS).
The reveal plays it at the exact moment the countdown hits zero and the name starts to appear. Brief: a soft dissolve whoosh into a cash-register "ka-ching" and falling coins.
Until this file exists, a built-in synthesized chime plays instead.
Keep a copy in the app's public/sounds folder too, so the app and the website sound the same.
